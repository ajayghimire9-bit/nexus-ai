# Future experience-transfer protocol (not run)

This is a separate study. It is not part of the capability-map screen
and must not be added silently to that run.

Compare four conditions on a predeclared source-to-target stream:

1. Relevant acquired experience
2. No acquired experience
3. Ordinary exact caching
4. Irrelevant experience

Charge acquisition. Measure retention. Use fresh target tasks that
were not used to acquire the experience. Keep ranking-policy training
paused unless a later protocol unlocks it. Learning-to-learn remains
unestablished until those controls pass.
